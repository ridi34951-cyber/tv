GyroLock next revision - conservative EIS trajectory

Changes:
- Keep the existing camera, DIGITAL/OPTICAL, zoom, Motion Photo, flash and build workflow code.
- Rework output stabilizer gating: stationary clips are detected using median and 90th-percentile angular-rate residuals after initial bias removal; stationary clips get a zero correction curve.
- Raise the per-axis dead-band and reject low-energy gyro samples before integration to reduce noise accumulation.
- Reduce trajectory correction gain and clamp, and smooth the correction curve more gently. This prioritizes natural movement over aggressive counter-motion and helps avoid left-right pumping.
- Preview path and native Vulkan shader are unchanged in this revision; focus is the saved-video stabilization path.
